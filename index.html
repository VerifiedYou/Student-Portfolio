QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

What does “verified” mean?

A verified item has been checked and approved by an authorized institution representative. The verifier and date stay visible on the record.

Can I control who sees my profile?

Yes. Choose what is public, private, or shared only with selected employers.

Do employers need an account?

No. Employers can open a shared profile link or scan a QR code to view verified records.

What if my institution is not yet on VerifiedYou?

Contact us and we will invite your institution to join so your records can be verified.
